From Nature to Your Home

Why We Started BeesBee

Over 80% of commercial honey found on supermarket shelves today is adulterated with corn syrup, sugar blends, or heated to unnatural temperatures that kill every live enzyme. We set out to change that.

🍃

Wild Floral Nectar Only

Our bees forage freely across deep mountain valleys and untouched wildflower canopies. We never feed our bees refined sugar syrups or artificial chemical supplements.

🛡️

NMR Tested Authenticity

Every harvest batch is subjected to Nuclear Magnetic Resonance (NMR) testing to verify zero sugar syrup adulteration, ensuring only 100% genuine floral honey touches your spoon.

🐝

Bee-First Ethics

Honeybees are vital pollinators for our planet's ecosystem. We collect only genuine excess honey from mature hives, ensuring bee colonies remain healthy and well-stocked throughout the year.

🫙

Zero Plastic Packaging

Honey is naturally mildly acidic. Cheap plastic squeeze bottles can leach chemicals over time. BeesBee packages exclusively in food-grade, heavy glass jars that seal in natural goodness.
