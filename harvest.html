Behind the Honey 🍯

We believe that when you buy raw honey, you have the right to see exactly where it comes from, how the bees are treated, and how it is bottled without industrial processing.

Raw Honey vs. Commercial Supermarket Honey

Why true unheated honey is completely different from commercial syrup bottles.

🍯 BeesBee Pure Natural Honey
  • ✓ Cold Extracted: Honey is extracted by gravity straining at ambient hive temperature.
  • ✓ Enzymes & Pollen Intact: Rich in diastase, invertase, and natural bio-active compounds.
  • ✓ Heavy Glass Jars: Prevents micro-plastics from reacting with honey's natural organic acids.
  • ✓ Crystallizes Naturally: Pure raw honey slowly sets over cold months—a hallmark of genuine honey.
🏭 Commercial Supermarket Honey
  • ✕ Ultra-Heated: Boiled at high heat to keep it liquid forever, destroying delicate enzymes.
  • ✕ Pollen Stripped: Ultra-filtered under high pressure to hide honey's floral and geographical origin.
  • ✕ Adulterated with Corn Syrup: Often blended with cheap high-fructose corn syrup or invert rice syrup.
  • ✕ Plastic Squeeze Bottles: Leach petrochemicals into honey during hot warehouse storage.
